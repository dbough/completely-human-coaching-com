How I Can Help

I help people discover themselves and build the life they're after.

This isn't a lofty promise. The work is real and it's often uncomfortable. We go at your pace, sit with what's hard, and do it without pretending to be anything other than what we are — human. That's not a side effect. It's the whole point.

People who reach out are carrying a lot. Some of them look like:

  • People who pour themselves into caring for others — kids, aging parents, anyone who needs them
  • Anyone in a career transition, job loss, or a toxic work environment
  • People looking for more meaning, or feeling a little lost
  • Anyone who wants to do deeper work on themselves but isn't sure where to start
  • People navigating relationships and wanting to show up differently
  • Anyone facing a health challenge — cancer, chronic illness, ADHD — and figuring out what life looks like now

You don't have to fit a profile. If something here is resonating, that's enough.

The Work

What we dig into together.

Every conversation is its own thing. But there are themes that come up again and again.

  • Where you're fulfilled — and where you're not
  • Your values, and how they actually show up (or don't) in your life
  • Your inner leader: learning to live with self-authority and self-acceptance
  • What really lights you up — and how to make more room for it

About

I coach from where I've actually been.

Hi, I'm Dan Bough. I started out flipping burgers and stocking shelves. Failed out of three colleges. Ended up in tech — 20 years engineering and leading teams, doing the hard inner work along the way. I know what it's like to succeed by most measures and still feel like something important is missing.

I'm a husband, a girl dad, a cancer survivor, a person of faith, and a trained Co-Active coach. I listen well, ask questions that matter, and genuinely care about the people I sit with. If you're looking for a thinking partner — someone to walk alongside you — we should talk.
