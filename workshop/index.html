The Idea

Most of us are moving too fast to notice

Too fast to notice where we're actually running low, or to hear the wiser part of ourselves underneath the noise. This workshop slows things down for 90 minutes to 2 hours and gives your group three simple, practical tools.

Three Tools

What attendees walk away with

  • The Wheel of Life

    A visual snapshot of where life feels full and where it doesn't, across nine areas most people never step back to compare side by side. Helps participants understand themselves better.

  • Inner Leader & Allies

    A way to hear the wiser, steadier part of yourself that's usually drowned out by daily noise. Helps participants tap into the self that already knows what it needs.

  • Inner Critic

    A way to name the voice that talks participants out of things, so they can build a healthier relationship with it instead of letting it quietly sabotage their growth.

How It Works

Interactive, but never required

Participants are invited to take part in each exercise, though never required to. Those who do tend to get the most out of it. Just quiet reflection, a few simple worksheets, and space to see their own lives a little more clearly than they did an hour ago.
