Who this is for

You're a tech manager, and something has shifted.

  • You just stepped into management.
  • A reorg, a layoff, or a process change landed on you overnight.
  • You got hard feedback from your boss or a direct report.
  • You're working toward a promotion.
  • You can feel burnout coming, or it's already here.

If any of that sounds like you, keep reading.

What I help with

Three things managers keep telling me.

“I need help making quicker decisions.”

You go back and forth, wait for one more piece of data, and the team waits on you.

“I need help delegating more of what's on my plate.”

You're doing your job and half of your team's, and it's hard to let go of any of it.

“I need help communicating better with my team.”

Expectations, company policies, the vision. You say it, and it doesn't land the way you meant.

You don't need another management book. You need a thought partner who's been there and will help you find your own answers.

What you walk away with

You leave doing these things differently.

  • You make decisions faster, and you stand behind them.
  • You hand off work with confidence, and get your time back.
  • You say what you mean in a way people hear.

You stay the expert on your own situation. I help you see it more clearly, decide, and act.

About

I've lived this.

I've spent 20+ years in tech, many of them leading teams. I started as a team of one and grew it to nine. Over and over I ran into the same three problems: making decisions quickly, delegating what was on my plate, and communicating clearly.

I wish I'd had a coach for those things much earlier. Eventually I became one.

Today I bring both sides to the work: what it's like to be the manager in the middle, and the training to coach someone through it. I'm a trained Co-Active coach, and I'd like to help people who are where I used to be.
