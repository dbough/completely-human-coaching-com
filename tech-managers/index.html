What I help with

Three things managers keep telling me.

“I need help making quicker decisions.”

You go back and forth, wait for one more piece of data, and the team waits on you.

“I need help delegating more of what's on my plate.”

You're doing your job and half of your team's, and it's hard to let go of any of it.

“I need help communicating better with my team.”

Expectations, company policies, the vision. You say it, and it doesn't land the way you meant.

You don't need another management book. You need a thought partner who's been there and will help you find your own answers.

Who I am

I've lived this.

My name is Dan Bough. I spent over 20 years working in tech, from startups to multibillion-dollar companies, with much of that time spent in engineering leadership.

Throughout my career, I struggled with many of the same challenges other tech leaders face. Making decisions with confidence, knowing when and how to delegate, communicating clearly, and navigating the constant pressure to deliver.

Too often, those challenges left me feeling overwhelmed, burned out, and unsure of what to do next. And sometimes, my teams felt the impact too.

Looking back, what I needed was someone who understood what I was going through and had the skills to help me work through it.

After more than 20 years in tech, I followed a calling to serve others and became a professionally trained life coach.

Now I'm realizing that I can bring those two worlds together. My experience in tech leadership gives me a firsthand understanding of the challenges managers face, and my coaching training gives me the skills to help them navigate those challenges for themselves.

I have an opportunity to be the coach I wish I'd had.
